          append-only
          manage · Production-grade Go persistence
        

        
A syllabus is immutable. Editing forks a new version.

        

          There is no PUT and no version field on the record — a Syllabus is append-only. To change
          intent you author a new version: a new record with a new id and its own SyllabusCreated
          event. v1 stays exactly as the cohort's binding left it. Change objectives or outcomes below, then
          fork v2.
