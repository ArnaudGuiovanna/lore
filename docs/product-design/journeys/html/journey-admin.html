identity & memberships

Roles are granted by membership.

A user is nothing until they hold a membership in this tenant. The membership carries the role; the client can never ask for one. When an IdP is wired in, LORE only verifies the token it issues.

⛬ OIDC verify-only boundary. When your identity provider issues tokens, LORE validates the RS256 signature and claims — it never mints user tokens itself. The subject is mapped to a membership; the membership's role is what the runtime enforces on every tenant route.
